# StartupGTM AI Partners Council System

**17 Strategic AI Partners** for solo founders — 9 executive partners + 8 challenge partners

## Executive Partners (Part 1)

| Partner | Focus | Supervision |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| CEO Partner | Vision, priorities, resource allocation | 1:5 |
| CMO Partner | Brand, positioning, narrative, growth | 1:5 |
| CTO Partner | Tech architecture, build vs. buy, roadmap | 1:5 |
| CFO Partner | Unit economics, pricing, runway, ROI | 1:5 |
| COO Partner | Operations, systems, SOPs, scalability | 1:5 |
| Head of Product | Roadmap, prioritization, PMF, features | 1:5 |
| Content Strategy | What to create, gaps, themes | 1:5 |
| Demand Gen | Channel mix, funnel, budget allocation | 1:5 |
| Intelligence Partner | Competitive intel, market trends | 1:3 (verify!) |

## Challenge Partners (Part 2)

| Partner | Focus |
|---------|-------|

## Key Insights

> "The founders who will win aren't the fastest content generators. They're the ones using AI to think better… not produce more."

**Execution AI:** "Write a blog post about X"  
**Strategic AI:** "What should our next 5 blog posts be about, given our gaps and goals?"

---

## Implementation

1. Copy the prompt for the partner you want
2. Customize [BRACKETED] sections with your specifics
3. Paste into Claude Projects or Custom GPTs as system instructions
4. Test with a real scenario before relying on it
5. Refine based on output quality

**Supervision Ratios:**
- 1:5 = Review ~1 out of every 5 outputs (strategic recommendations)
- 1:3 = Tighter oversight (higher fabrication risk or personal stakes)
