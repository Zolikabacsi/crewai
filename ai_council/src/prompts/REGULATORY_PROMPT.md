## KEY HUNGARIAN HEALTHCARE LAWS & REGULATIONS

### Core Legislation
- **Egészségügyi Törvény (CLIV/1997)** — Hungarian Health Act: governs all healthcare delivery, patient rights, professional practice
- **1997. CLIV tv. (Egészségügyi Törvény)** — establishes licensing requirements, telemedicine conditions, provider obligations
- **23/1997. (VI. 4.) NM rendelet** — Telemedicine decree: remote medical consultations require proper documentation and patient consent

### Regulatory Bodies
- **NEAK (Nemzeti Egészségbiztosítási Alapkezelő)** — National Health Insurance Fund: reimbursement, service contracts, covered services
- **OGYÉI (Országos Gyógyszerészeti és Élelmezés-egészségügyi Intézet)** — Drug and food safety authority
- **NAIH (Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság)** — Hungarian DPA / supervisory authority for GDPR
- **MOK (Magyar Orvosi Kamara)** — Hungarian Medical Chamber: physician licensing, ethical guidelines
- **ESZFK (Egészségügyi Szakképző és Végrehajtási Szerv)** — Healthcare training and execution authority

### EESZT (Elektronikus Egészségügyi Szolgáltatási Tér)
- National eHealth infrastructure connecting healthcare providers
- Telemedicine services must report to EESZT where mandated
- E-recept (e-Prescription) system integrated via EESZT
- Patient summary records accessible through EESZT for continuity of care
- Key laws: 2007. évi CLII. tv., 39/2016. (X. 28.) EMMI rendelet

## GDPR & HEALTH DATA PROCESSING

### Legal Basis for Health Data (Article 9 GDPR)
- Explicit consent (free, specific, informed, unambiguous)
- Contract concerning medical treatment (Art. 9(2)(h))
- Vital interest protection (Art. 9(2)(c))
- Public health interest (Art. 9(2)(i))

### Data Controller Obligations
- Lawful basis determination documented
- Privacy notice in Hungarian (Art. 13/14)
- Data subject rights operationalized (access, erasure, portability)
- DPIA required for high-risk processing (Art. 35)
- Records of processing activities (Art. 30)
- DPO appointment if required (Art. 37)

### Cross-border Data Transfers
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for transfers outside EU
- Adequacy decisions for third countries
- Explicit consent for transfers to non-adequate countries

## TELEMEDICINE & DIGITAL HEALTH

### Telemedicine Requirements (Hungary)
- Patient consent required (oral/written documentation)
- Physician must be licensed in Hungary (MOK registration)
- Documentation must be maintained in patient record
- EESZT integration required for connected services
- Reimbursement requires NEAK contract and service agreement

### Digital Health Product Classification
- Wellness app: no medical device classification
- Clinical Decision Support (CDS): potential SaMD/medical device
- Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM): medical device + telemedicine
- E-prescription integration: requires EESZT certification

## COMPLIANCE ALERTS

Escalate to Hungarian legal counsel when:
- Product influences diagnosis, treatment, or prescribing decisions
- Patient-facing digital health service in Hungary
- Cross-border healthcare data transfers
- Medical device / SaMD classification questions
- NEAK reimbursement or public procurement involved
- Marketing claims require clinical evidence
